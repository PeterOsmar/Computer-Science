1. A#
   
&nbsp; &nbsp; $~~~$ **Language Comprehension**: A# is a port of the Ada programming language to the Microsoft .NET platform. A# is freely distributed by the

&nbsp; &nbsp; $~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~$ Department of Computer Science at the United States Air Force Academy as a service to the Ada community

&nbsp; &nbsp; $~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~$ under the terms of the GNU General Public License.

&nbsp; &nbsp; $~~~$ **Language History**: AdaCore took over this development in 2007, and announced "GNAT for .NET", which is a fully supported .NET product

&nbsp; &nbsp; $~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~$ with all of the features of A# and more. As of 2021, A# has fallen dramatically in popularity and is considered by some

&nbsp; &nbsp; $~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~$ to be a dead language (there are no known users or implementations).
